Signature Lounge is the premier cocktail bar inside the Sheraton Addis — the city's flagship luxury hotel — and has long been the meeting point of choice for diplomats, senior government officials, visiting dignitaries, and the upper tier of Addis Ababa's social scene. The combination of impeccable service, a world-class bar programme, and the gravitas of the Sheraton setting makes it unlike anywhere else in the city.

## The Bar

The cocktail menu is the most technically accomplished in Addis — built by internationally trained mixologists using imported spirits, house-made bitters, infused syrups, and fresh garnishes. The wine list is extensive, with serious representation from France, Italy, and South Africa. Cigar service is available on request.

## The Atmosphere

A sophisticated lounge setting with low leather seating, warm lighting, and live piano from Thursday to Saturday. The room comfortably holds 80 guests without feeling crowded. The dress code is enforced: smart casual minimum, formal preferred.

## What to Order

- **Habesha Negroni** — Ethiopian honey spirit in place of gin; a local twist on the classic
- **Ethiopian Highlands** — house whisky sour with tej and cardamom bitters
- **Wine by the glass** — the sommelier's selections change weekly

## Visitor Tips

- Reserve a table on weekends — walk-in availability is limited after 8 PM.
- The Sheraton lobby bar (adjacent) is a quieter option for afternoon drinks before the lounge opens.
- Business casual is the informal standard; jeans and sneakers are not permitted.
