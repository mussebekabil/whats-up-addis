Kategna is one of Addis Ababa's most beloved contemporary Ethiopian restaurants — a modern take on traditional cooking that has earned a devoted following among the city's young professionals, returning diaspora, and discerning visitors. The name "Kategna" refers to the crispy, slightly charred edge of injera that cooks against the mitad (clay cooking disc), a cherished bite-sized treat in Ethiopian culture.

## Philosophy and Approach

Kategna was founded on the idea that Ethiopian cuisine deserves to be showcased with the same care and creativity as the world's great culinary traditions. The kitchen sources seasonal, locally grown ingredients and takes time to prepare dishes the traditional way — long-simmered stews, hand-ground spice blends, and injera fermented for the perfect amount of time.

## Signature Dishes

### Breakfast

Kategna excels at breakfast — a serious meal in Ethiopian culture:

- **Chechebsa** — torn flatbread dressed with niter kibbeh (spiced clarified butter) and berbere
- **Firfir with eggs** — injera pieces tossed with soft scrambled eggs and spiced butter
- **Genfo** — thick porridge topped with a well of spiced butter and berbere

### Lunch and Dinner

- **Lamb Tibs** — tender cubes of lamb shoulder seared in a clay pot with rosemary, jalapeño, and onion
- **Timatim Fitfit** — a refreshing tomato and injera salad dressed with lime
- **Category Combo** — a curated selection of the kitchen's best dishes arranged on injera for sharing

### Coffee and Pastries

Kategna's coffee corner serves speciality Ethiopian coffee using beans sourced from Yirgacheffe, Sidama, and Jimma, paired with homemade pastries and traditional cookies.

## Visitor Tips

- The breakfast service is exceptional — arrive by 8 AM for the freshest injera.
- Tables fill quickly at lunchtime; reservations recommended.
- The casual outdoor seating area is perfect for people-watching.
